OBOSA OGIEVA

Creator of Ifueko

“Here’s to all the things I see with my eyes closed.”

I started my career as a fashion illustrator working with Amy Aghomi, where, among many other creations, I designed Davido and Chioma’s black-gold wedding outfit.

I believe clothes should fit people, not the other way around, so Ifueko is a 100% couture brand.

My latest collection, Las Gidi, captures the entrepreneurial spirit of a restless Lagos woman who shapes the world to fit her vision. She has both ambition and taste. She is fearlessly creative. Enjoy.
